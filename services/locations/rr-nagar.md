# Cosmic Lashes And Nails RR Nagar

Cosmic Lashes And Nails RR Nagar is the original studio where the CLAN journey began. As the first branch of Cosmic Lashes And Nails, RR Nagar represents the foundation of the brand and the beginning of its nail and beauty service journey.

## Nail and Beauty Services in RR Nagar

The RR Nagar studio offers a range of professional nail and beauty services for different styles and occasions. Services include nail extensions, nail art, 3D nail art, gel polish, eyelash extensions, manicure and pedicure, bridal nails and customized nail designs.

## Nail Extensions in RR Nagar

Clients can choose customized nail extensions based on their preferred length, shape, colour and finish. Nail extensions can also be paired with creative nail art for a personalized look.

## Nail Art in RR Nagar

The studio offers nail art ranging from simple and elegant designs to detailed creative styles. Clients can discuss their preferred colours, patterns and reference designs with the nail artist.

## Eyelash Extensions in RR Nagar

Eyelash extension services are available for clients looking to enhance their eye appearance with a customized lash style. The preferred look can be discussed before the service.

## Bridal Nails in RR Nagar

Brides can choose customized nail extensions and nail art designed around their wedding outfit, colours and personal style. The studio also caters to other special occasions and celebrations.
