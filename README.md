## About Cosmic Lashes And Nails

Cosmic Lashes And Nails brings together professional nail care and beauty services for clients looking for elegant everyday styles, creative nail art and occasion-ready beauty looks.

Our services are designed around individual preferences. Clients can choose from different nail shapes, colours, finishes and designs, while our beauty professionals help create a look that suits their personal style and occasion.

## Nail Extensions

Nail extensions are ideal for clients who want added length, shape and a polished finish. Cosmic Lashes And Nails offers customized extension styles that can be combined with different colours, finishes and nail art designs.

## Nail Art

From minimal designs to detailed and creative styles, nail art can be customized according to your preferred colour palette, occasion or reference design. Clients can bring inspiration images and discuss their preferred look with the nail artist.

## Eyelash Extensions

Eyelash extensions are designed to enhance the appearance of the eyes while creating a more defined beauty look. The service can be personalized according to the desired style and overall appearance.

## Bridal Nails

For weddings and other important occasions, customized nail designs can complement the overall bridal look. From elegant finishes to detailed nail art, clients can choose a design based on their outfit, theme and personal preference.
