# Cosmic Lashes And Nails HSR Layout

Cosmic Lashes And Nails HSR Layout brings professional nail and beauty services to clients looking for creative nail designs, polished finishes and personalized beauty experiences. The studio is part of the growing CLAN network and follows the brand's focus on detailed nail artistry and professional service.

## Nail and Beauty Services in HSR Layout

The HSR Layout studio offers services including nail extensions, nail art, 3D nail art, gel polish, eyelash extensions, manicure and pedicure, bridal nails and customized nail designs.

## Nail Extensions in HSR Layout

Clients can select nail extensions according to their preferred length, shape, colour and overall style. Extensions can be paired with different finishes and customized nail art for everyday looks or special occasions.

## Nail Art in HSR Layout

From clean and minimal designs to detailed creative nail art, clients can choose a style that matches their personality or occasion. Reference images can also be used to communicate the preferred design to the nail artist.

## Gel Polish and Nail Care

Gel polish provides an option for clients who want a polished nail finish with a choice of colours and styles. Manicure and pedicure services are also available for regular nail care and grooming.

## Eyelash Extensions in HSR Layout

Clients can enhance their overall beauty look with customized eyelash extensions. The preferred lash appearance can be discussed with the beauty professional before the service.

## Bridal Nails in HSR Layout

Brides and clients attending special occasions can choose customized nail extensions and nail art based on their outfit, event theme and personal preferences.
