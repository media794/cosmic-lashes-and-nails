# Cosmic Lashes And Nails RR Nagar

Cosmic Lashes And Nails RR Nagar is the original studio where the CLAN journey began. As the first branch of Cosmic Lashes And Nails, RR Nagar represents the foundation of the brand and the beginning of its nail and beauty service journey.

## Nail and Beauty Services in RR Nagar

The studio offers nail extensions, nail art, 3D nail art, gel polish, eyelash extensions, manicure and pedicure, bridal nails and customized nail designs.

## Personalized Nail Services

Clients can choose different nail shapes, colours, finishes and designs based on their personal style. Nail artists can also work with reference images and customized design ideas.

## Bridal and Occasion Nails

The studio provides customized nail looks for weddings, celebrations and other special occasions, from elegant designs to detailed nail art.
